Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with BookForgeKDP. This policy explains when refunds are available for payments made through Razorpay.

1. Free Plan

The free plan includes 2 manuscript exports at no cost. No payment is involved, so no refund applies.

2. Starter (Per-Export) Purchases

If a Starter export fails technically (for example, the export produces a corrupted or unusable file and we cannot fix it within 7 days of your report), you are eligible for a full refund of that export.

3. Author Lifetime (One-Time Purchase)

You may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not exported more than one book with the plan. After 7 days, or after substantial use, refunds are at our discretion.

4. Agency/Pro Monthly Subscription

  • First subscription payment: full refund within 7 days of the first charge if you are not satisfied.
  • Renewal payments: refundable within 48 hours of the renewal charge if you have not used exports in that billing period.
  • Cancellation: you can cancel anytime from the app or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future charges; your Pro features remain active until the end of the current billing period. No prorated refunds for partial periods.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email support@bookforgekdp.com with your registered email, payment reference (you can find it in your payment confirmation or Account panel), and the reason for the refund. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days via Razorpay.

6. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Change of mind after 7 days
  • Refusal to read or follow the app's export instructions
  • Account termination for violation of our Terms & Conditions

7. Contact

Refund questions? Reach us at support@bookforgekdp.com.
