Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

BookForgeKDP (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the BookForgeKDP web application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign up with Google or email and password, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture (if provided).
  • Manuscripts and content: We store the manuscripts, book titles, chapter text, and formatting settings you upload or create so we can provide the formatting and export features.
  • Payment information: Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank details on our servers. We store only the payment reference ID, amount, currency, and plan purchased.
  • Usage data: Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited may be collected to keep the service working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store and format your manuscripts
  • To process subscription and one-time payments through Razorpay
  • To send transactional emails such as sign-in codes and password resets
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay — to process payments (Razorpay acts as an independent data controller for payment data it collects)
  • Google — if you sign in with Google, Google handles your authentication
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting and database providers needed to run the service
  • Authorities, where required by law

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your manuscripts and account data are retained while your account is active. You can delete individual books at any time from the dashboard, or delete your entire account (including all books, chapters, subscriptions, and payment records) from the Account panel. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), server-side signature verification for payments, and access controls to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

6. Children's Privacy

BookForgeKDP is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including India's DPDP Act, GDPR, and other applicable laws), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app, or contact us for help.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at abookforge@gmail.com or via the Contact page.
